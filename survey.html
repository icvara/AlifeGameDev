founding survey

Help shape the community

open until 27 Nov 2026 · · about 5 minutes

Invitations went out to developers and creators whose work stood out, but anyone working on artificial life games is welcome to answer.

The answers decide whether the community is worth building, and what it should look like. Good to know:

  • Answers stay private. They're only used to shape the community, never shared publicly or used for anything else.
  • Answering is no commitment. Joining is a separate decision, later.
  • You can ask for your answers to be deleted at any time.
  • You can suggest other ALife game devs or creators who'd be a good fit.

After it closes:

  1. Anonymised results are shared with everyone who took part.
  2. Invitations are sent to those who are interested.
