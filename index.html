a community in the making

A community for people who make games with artificial life.

ALife Game Dev is a small, non-profit community for game developers and creators working with artificial life: evolving creatures, living ecosystems, emergent worlds.

The people building these games are spread across many engines, forums and corners of the internet. The aim is to bring them together in one place to share progress, struggles and discoveries, support each other, and share an enthusiasm for artificial life.

The community is still taking shape. The founding survey is open until 27 November 2026.

01The community

Many people in this field work alone, on projects that are hard to explain to anyone outside it. The community is meant as a place to post a strange thing your creatures did and talk it over with people who see why it's interesting.

What it's for:

  • sharing progress, devlogs and screenshots
  • asking for help when the simulation won't behave
  • passing around papers, tools and techniques
  • small, friendly, and non-profit

The details (where it lives, how often members meet, what happens there) will be decided from the survey results.
